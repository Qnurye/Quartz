# Hey There! 👋

This is my so-called Digital Garden (Second Brain, PKMS, Knowledge Base, or whatever you prefer to call it:). Compared to "articles," which demand significant time and energy, "notes"—being shorter and more cohesive—offer much less resistance for me to produce. This is especially true given that my Obsidian is already filled with a bunch of messy, random stuff.

## What's in Here?

To be honest, I don't really know either. I might even just use this as a transit hub for sharing documents; it's pretty random. Feel free to wander around!
